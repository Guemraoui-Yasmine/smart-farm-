🌱 Our Vision

Water stress is one of the most critical challenges facing modern agriculture, particularly in North Africa and arid regions worldwide. SmartFarm was born from the conviction that data-driven, technology-enabled farming can dramatically reduce water consumption while increasing crop yields. By combining IoT sensors, real-time weather data, and intelligent advisory systems, we aim to make precision agriculture accessible to every farmer — regardless of budget or technical expertise.
